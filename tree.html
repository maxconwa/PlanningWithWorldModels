    Every frame in the tree is drawn by the world model's decoder, including the root, which decodes the RSSM state after the real frame. Nodes below it are states the search imagined, sampled once per node. Each edge is the action taken from its parent, with the simulations that passed through it (N) and any predicted reward (r). V is the mean value the search backed up through a node.
